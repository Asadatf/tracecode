| Behaviour change, however small (copy, limits, flow) | Update the spec in the same PR. |
| Refactor, dependency bump, chore, CI, styling with no change to behaviour | No spec. |
| A choice with real trade-offs (library, data shape, security posture) | Write an ADR. |
